01 / A SOUNDTRACK WITH PRESENCE

Less alt-tabbing.
More being there.

Keep your music close without leaving the game. Nocturne brings synchronized lyrics and desktop playback into Minecraft, with enough control to make it feel like part of your world.

THE WORDS, IN THEIR ELEMENT

A line.

Your world.

World-space lyrics, a screen-space fallback and word-level karaoke when timestamps are available. Fine-tune the timing yourself in the lyric workspace.

02 / MAKE IT YOURS

Set the mood.
Keep the control.

From quiet captions to a little more spectacle. Preview themes, choose your colors and put the lyrics exactly where you want them.

CONTEXT AWARE

A different kind of focus.

Separate profiles for exploration, combat, menus and screenshots. Hide the lyrics during chat or inventory, on your terms.

A QUIETER OPTION

Turn the intensity down.

Independent motion and effect controls, readable backplates and a photosensitivity-friendly preset for Nocturne’s own visuals.

KEEP THE MUSIC GOING

Already playing?
We’re listening.

Automatic desktop detection is the default. Change tracks with configurable in-game shortcuts and switch players using your priorities.

Controls depend on what your player exposes. Optional authenticated integrations unlock supported extras; they’re never required for desktop detection.

03 / PRESS PLAY

Three steps.
Then, tune out.

  1. 01

    Choose your build.

    Select your Minecraft version and a Nocturne release. Check the listed Java and Fabric requirements.

  2. 02

    Drop it into mods.

    Install Fabric Loader and Fabric API for that Minecraft version, then put the Nocturne JAR in your instance’s mods folder.

  3. 03

    Play something. Press N.

    Start your desktop music player, launch Minecraft and open Nocturne’s Studio. Lyrics depend on source availability; a built-in demo is ready to try.
