# Site assets

All gameplay and interface images are original Nocturne captures copied from the repository’s `docs/` directory. The world and shader captures use Nocturne’s built-in demonstration track. They are actual captures, not generated gameplay or a representation of a live music account.

`icon.png` is the mod’s existing icon. `smooth.ttf` and `smooth_bold.ttf` are the mod’s bundled Hanken Grotesk fonts, used under the accompanying [SIL Open Font License](OFL-HankenGrotesk.txt). No third-party image libraries, remotely hosted fonts, analytics or trackers are used.
