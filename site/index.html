  2. 02

    Drop it into mods.

    Install the listed loader and dependencies, then place one Nocturne JAR in your instance’s mods folder.

  3. 03

    Press play. Press N.

    Start your desktop player and open the Studio in-game. Try the built-in demo first; lyric coverage depends on the source.
